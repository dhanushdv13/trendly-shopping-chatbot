# Trendly Store Policy

## Returns
At Trendly, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. 
- You have **30 days** from the date of delivery to return your items for a full refund.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with all tags attached.
- **Exceptions:** Items marked as "Sale", "Clearance", or any discounted item are final sale and are not eligible for returns.

## Exchanges
If you need a different size, color, or style, we offer free exchanges!
- Exchanges must be initiated within **30 days** of delivery.
- Sale or clearance items cannot be exchanged.
- If the new item is priced differently, you will either be refunded the difference or charged the remaining balance.

## Refund Timelines
- Once we receive your returned item, please allow 3-5 business days for our warehouse to inspect and process the return.
- After processing, refunds are issued back to the original payment method. 
- It may take an additional 5-7 business days for the funds to appear on your bank or credit card statement, depending on your financial institution.

## Shipping Timelines
- **Standard Shipping:** 5-7 business days.
- **Express Shipping:** 2-3 business days.
- Orders are processed within 1-2 business days before they are shipped out. You will receive a tracking number once your order has shipped.

## Missing or Damaged Packages
- If your package is marked as delivered but you have not received it, please check with neighbors or your local post office.
- If the package is still missing after 48 hours from the marked delivery time, please escalate to our support team for an investigation.
- For items arriving damaged, please contact support immediately with your order ID and a description of the damage so we can send a replacement or issue a refund.
